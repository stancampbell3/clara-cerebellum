## 2. Composed analysts

- A parent config `assistant:clara:<hash>` with a lexical router and five `owned` child Rituals, generated from the existing analyst `.pl` files plus a small adapter (`reasoned_response/3` wrapping `assistant_turn/5`, returning JSON). The `.pl` files stay the source of truth. **Decisions (Stan):** routing only, lexical rules first, keep the `.pl` files and generate the configs.
- Routing order: decision cues to deliberative, brainstorm cues to id, do-a-task cues to ego, short chit-chat to terse, everything else to progressive. A child failure falls through to progressive, then to the old single-analyst path, so the assistant never goes down.
- Configs are owned by a system identity (`system:assistant`), versioned by content hash, recreated when the hash changes, persistent (resume after restart), and warmed at startup.
- Two S7 gaps fixed as prerequisites: `perform()` now forwards the conversation context to child Rituals, and its cycle budget scales with the patience of a consulting entry node.
- Verified live: all five routes, history reaching children, restart resume, self-healing rebuild. Cost is about one extra hop (~2 s) per turn; cold-model first turns fail on both composed and direct paths (see section 7).
- Not in this cut: background legs inside child Performances, an LLM intent classifier, making `clara` the default, Cobbler editing of generated configs. Design: `docs/composed_analysts.md`.

## 5. Kafka cleanup and who is responsible

**Decision (Stan):** Dis owns topic deletion; participants notice the terminated state on poll and clean up their own state.
- **Dis** (`RitualRegistry::reap_topics`, thread `ritual-topic-reaper`): deletes a terminated Ritual's topic after `ritual_topic_grace_seconds` (600), sweeping every 300 s. With `ritual_topic_reap_orphans` (default true) it also deletes `{domain}.ritual.{uuid}` topics the registry does not know, once seen on two consecutive sweeps (so a topic created a moment before its registry entry is safe). Active Rituals and non-ritual topics are never touched. Grace 0 disables it.
- **FieryPit** (`goat/app/participant_reaper.py`, every 60 s, `RITUAL_PARTICIPANT_REAP_INTERVAL_SECONDS`): polls Dis per joined Ritual; on `terminated` or 404 it stops the consumer, frees the evaluator slot and forgets the participant. A Dis outage does nothing.
- Consumer groups are left to Kafka, which expires empty ones.
- Live: the first sweep after deploy deleted 300 terminated Rituals' topics with 0 errors; Kafka shows no ephemeral topics without a live Ritual.
- Existing precedent: Dis already had the `CarrionPicker` for Coire store rows; nothing had covered Kafka.
